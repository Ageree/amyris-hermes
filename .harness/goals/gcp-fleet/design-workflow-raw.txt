gs://hermes-fleet-state/tenants/<userId>/\n```\n\n### 2.2 The cold-start → serve → idle-reap loop\n1. **Cold-start.** Tenant message hits the `.convex.site` webhook → resolves `(channel,address)→userId` (verified binding only) → enqueues with `userId/channel/replyTarget` → `requestInstanceInternal(userId)` upserts `agentInstances{desired:running,status:provisioning}`.\n2. **Launch.** Within one ~3s poll the controller `_decide`→`launch`: `rehydrate(userId)` from GCS (empty first time, fail-soft) → `docker run` scoped container (`_build_env` + bind-mount `/data/tenants/<userId>`) → `claimInstanceForLaunch` (atomic; loser tears down). First `heartbeat` flips `provisioning→running`.\n3. **Serve.** Container polls `claimNextForUser(userId)` (server-side `by_status_user` scope — physically cannot claim another tenant's rows), runs the Hermes turn, replies to the claimed row's **own** `replyTarget` on the claimed `channel` (iMessage via Sendblue, Telegram via the Telegram channel). `complete`/`fail` only flip a row that is still `processing` (at-most-once finalize).\n4. **Idle-reap.** Free tier: `reapIdleInstances` cron flips `desired=stopped` after 30 min idle. Controller `_do_stop`: `docker stop` → `mirror(userId)` to GCS → `markStopped`. Paid tier stays warm. Next message cold-starts again and `rehydrate` restores prior state.\n\n### 2.3 Channel-agnostic per-message routing (already correct; do not regress)\nRouting is per **message**, not per worker config. The scoped container is **never** pinned to an operator number (`ALLOWED_USER_NUMBER` deliberately absent from `_build_env`, controller.py:218-220). A tenant row with a missing `replyTarget` is **failed** (not sent to the operator). This is the multi-tenant keystone — any change to `_build_env` or worker routing must preserve the omission and the fail-closed behavior.\n\n---\n\n## 3. ORDERED implementation punch-list\n\n### 3a.
